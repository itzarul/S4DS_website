# S4DS website

The site presents TCET's Society 4 Data Science student community, its activities, and contact details. Visitors explore the interactive introduction, browse archive photographs, and contact the listed officers.

The existing identity is black and electric blue with technical typography, interactive 3D artwork, and scroll-led transitions. Preserve the established hero and archive when making footer changes. Support desktop and mobile layouts and reduced motion.

The contact footer adapts the user-supplied Desktop/footer installation: luminous rings, floating textured panels, and a stepped pedestal surround a live code-rendered butterfly replacing the source scene's stone. A single viewport pins while further scrolling fades in the initially hidden butterfly, raises the S4DS letters in reverse order, and reveals the other text sequentially. Desktop places CONNECT WITH US and the large S4DS home link at lower left, the scene in the center, and officer contacts on the right. Mobile fits the contacts above the scene and the wordmark below it within the same pinned viewport. Both officer addresses are usable email links, and the complete text remains available to assistive technology throughout the visual reveal. The old hands, asterisk, and butterfly video are retired from the footer runtime. Lazy loading, offscreen and hidden-tab suspension, bounded rendering, and a pause control support the interactive scene and shared particle atmosphere. Reduced motion shows the complete footer in one viewport without the extra scroll runway. The current direction contract and confirmed contact addresses are recorded in `docs/connect-footer-brief.md`; implementation details are recorded in DESIGN.md.
