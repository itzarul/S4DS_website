# Navigation — October 4

Home is the complete hero/archive/contact page. Team, Events and Gallery are separate static HTML routes with deliberately blank bodies, shared navigation, accessible page names, and dark navy, violet and teal backgrounds respectively. Their content is intentionally pending.

Contact opens the fully revealed footer on Home, including from another route, and bypasses the opening animation on direct contact arrival. Home returns to the top. Standard links preserve browser history, reloads, modified clicks and open-in-new-tab behavior.

Shared native view transitions reveal the destination through five offset scan bands over 1100ms, with a subtle retreat of the previous page. Browsers without view-transition support navigate normally; reduced motion disables the animation. The old raster bleed atlas is no longer used by navigation.

The archive ground fades to the footer's blue-black ground at its bottom edge. The footer's atmosphere fades up from that same color.

Footer typography follows the latest reference: KEEP IN SYNC above two-line CONNECT / WITH US on desktop, white Nippo CONTACTS, and the original officer contacts. The intro block is hidden on mobile. Reverse wordmark reveal starts at 8% scroll progress with 17% stagger from the rightmost S toward D, 4, and leftmost S; each letter rises across 25% of progress.

Freeze console paragraph starts “S4DS is a student-driven community”; the redundant “Society” is removed.
